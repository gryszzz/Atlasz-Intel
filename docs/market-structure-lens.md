# YSZ Market Structure Lens

The Market Structure Lens exists to make markets easier to read without reducing them to a buy/sell light.

## The five-second read

Every asset should be understandable through a small number of questions:

1. **Trend** — Is price advancing, declining, ranging, or transitioning?
2. **Liquidity** — Is liquidity improving, tightening, stable, or fragmented?
3. **Volatility** — Is the market calm, expanding, or in a high-volatility regime?
4. **Positioning** — Is positioning balanced, changing, or crowded?
5. **Flows** — Are observable flows moving in, out, rotating, mixed, or unavailable?
6. **World exposure** — Which policy, macro, commodity, infrastructure, ownership, or blockchain events currently intersect the asset?
7. **Invalidation** — What observable change would make the current structural read weaker?

The interface should answer these before showing secondary indicators.

## Progressive disclosure

### Layer 1 — plain language
Example:

> Advancing trend · tightening liquidity · elevated volatility · positioning becoming crowded.

### Layer 2 — why
Show the few underlying measurements responsible for the classification.

### Layer 3 — raw data
Charts, distributions, percentiles, options surfaces, source trails, and timestamps.

This makes the product readable for a fast scan while preserving quant depth for inspection.

## No fake certainty

YSZ should never convert structure into:
- BUY
- SELL
- guaranteed target
- guaranteed reversal
- prediction presented as fact

Instead it can surface:
- supportive conditions
- conflicting conditions
- unusual conditions
- structural exposure
- observed reaction
- invalidation questions

The user remains the decision-maker.

## Market map

A useful asset screen should read top-to-bottom:

```text
ASSET
current state

STRUCTURE
trend · liquidity · volatility · positioning · flows

WHY NOW
material events and abnormal changes

EXPOSURE
policy · macro · commodity · company · geography · blockchain

REACTION
what price/volume/volatility actually did

TENSIONS
where signals disagree

INVALIDATION
what would weaken the current read

PROOF
source trails and timestamps
```

## Visual grammar

Avoid dozens of indicators.

Use:
- one structure strip
- one clean price/volume chart
- one distribution/anomaly view
- one event timeline
- one exposure graph

Every visible metric should answer a question.

## Entry/exit thinking without trade signals

For users evaluating their own trade, YSZ can help organize the questions:

**Before entry**
- Is the trend aligned or transitional?
- Is liquidity supportive or deteriorating?
- Is volatility unusually high?
- Is positioning already crowded?
- Are flows confirming or conflicting?
- Is a material world event approaching?
- What would invalidate the thesis?

**While holding**
- Did the structure improve, weaken, or remain unchanged?
- Did the original thesis receive confirming or conflicting evidence?
- Did volatility or liquidity move outside the expected regime?
- Did ownership, policy, macro, or blockchain context materially change?

**Before exit**
- Has the original structural thesis been invalidated?
- Has positioning become extreme?
- Did observed flows reverse?
- Did the event catalyst resolve?
- Has the asset moved into a different regime?

YSZ supplies context and evidence. It does not make the financial decision.
